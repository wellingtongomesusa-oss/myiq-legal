Privacy Policy — MyIQ Poster

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who uses this app

MyIQ Poster is used only by its owner, on the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts (Clutch Cuts and Lance Brabo). No other people sign in to it.

What data it accesses

  • Basic account info (account name, avatar, ID) to confirm which account a video goes to.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos the owner created.
  • Statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time).

How it is used

Only to publish the owner's clips and to measure how they perform. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer and in the owner's private automation environment. Statistics are stored in private files. Nothing is published except the videos themselves.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services.

Deleting data

Revoking access stops all use. The owner deletes stored tokens and statistics on request or when the tool is retired.
